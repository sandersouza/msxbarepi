-   Any **port to a new platform** or significant change requires prior
    contact with you.
-   Any **commercial use** requires a specific licensing agreement.

### Objective Request

I kindly request:

1.  Explicit authorization to port fMSX to a **bare metal Raspberry Pi
    environment**, including necessary adaptations to run without an
    operating system.
2.  Guidance on the conditions for:
    -   Source and/or binary distribution;
    -   Proper author attribution;
    -   License inclusion and placement;
    -   Public repository publication (if permitted).

### Technical Context and Scope

The project aims to maintain compatibility and behavior equivalent to
the original fMSX, including:

-   Support for ROM, disk, and cassette media formats.
-   MSX-BASIC initialization when no valid image is loaded.
-   Persistence mechanisms such as RTC, CMOS, and SRAM.
-   User-supplied BIOS and ROM files only (no redistribution).
-   Documentation of known limitations and unsupported hardware.

I also commit to:

-   Providing clear and visible attribution to you as the original
    author.
-   Including the LICENSE file without modification.
-   Avoiding commercial use without a separate agreement.
-   Ensuring users are responsible for legal BIOS and ROM usage.

Thank you for your time and for your outstanding work on fMSX.
