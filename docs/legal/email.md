# Request and Authorization -- Bare Metal Raspberry Pi Port of fMSX

## 1. Authorization Request

Dear Mr. Marat Fayzullin,

My name is Alessander Goulart (Brazil). I am currently developing a
**bare metal port (without an operating system)** for the **Raspberry
Pi**, aiming to run an MSX emulator based on **fMSX 6.0 (21-Feb-2021)**.
This work is derived from your original code and also references an
existing port targeting a specific execution API (as described in the
attached README). Therefore, I am contacting you to formally request
your **authorization** to proceed with this port and distribute the
result under the conditions you define.

According to your LICENSE, I understand and fully respect that:

-   The fMSX source code is **not public domain** and cannot be copied
    or modified without proper credit and permission.
-   Any **port to a new platform** or significant change requires prior
    contact with you.
-   Any **commercial use** requires a specific licensing agreement.

### Objective Request

I kindly request:

1.  Explicit authorization to port fMSX to a **bare metal Raspberry Pi
    environment**, including necessary adaptations to run without an
    operating system.
2.  Guidance on the conditions for:
    -   Source and/or binary distribution;
    -   Proper author attribution;
    -   License inclusion and placement;
    -   Public repository publication (if permitted).

### Technical Context and Scope

The project aims to maintain compatibility and behavior equivalent to
the original fMSX, including:

-   Support for ROM, disk, and cassette media formats.
-   MSX-BASIC initialization when no valid image is loaded.
-   Persistence mechanisms such as RTC, CMOS, and SRAM.
-   User-supplied BIOS and ROM files only (no redistribution).
-   Documentation of known limitations and unsupported hardware.

I also commit to:

-   Providing clear and visible attribution to you as the original
    author.
-   Including the LICENSE file without modification.
-   Avoiding commercial use without a separate agreement.
-   Ensuring users are responsible for legal BIOS and ROM usage.

Thank you for your time and for your outstanding work on fMSX.

Best regards,\
Alessander Goulart\
Brazil

## 2. Authorization Response

Dear Mr. Goulart,

Thank you for your message and for your interest in the fMSX project. I
appreciate your effort to contact me in advance and your clear intention
to comply with the licensing terms.

I hereby grant you permission to proceed with your **bare metal port of
fMSX for the Raspberry Pi**, under the conditions outlined below. This
authorization applies to both development and distribution of your port,
provided that the original project and its license are fully respected.

### License and Attribution Requirements

Please ensure that the following conditions are strictly observed:

1.  **Proper Attribution**\
    The fMSX emulator is my original work and is not in the public
    domain. Any port, modification, or derivative work must clearly and
    visibly credit the original project and author.

2.  **License Inclusion**\
    The original LICENSE file must be included in full and unmodified in
    any distributed version of your project.

3.  **Reference to the Original Project**\
    Your project must explicitly state that it is based on the fMSX
    emulator and provide a reference to the original work.

4.  **Non-Commercial Use**\
    This authorization is limited to non-commercial purposes. Any
    commercial use requires a separate agreement.

5.  **BIOS and ROM Responsibility**\
    BIOS files, ROM images, or copyrighted software must not be
    distributed.

6.  **Respect for the Spirit of the Project**\
    The port should support preservation, research, and enjoyment of the
    MSX platform.

### Additional Notes

Your bare metal approach for the Raspberry Pi is an interesting and
technically valuable direction. Running fMSX without an operating system
may improve performance and hardware integration. I encourage you to
document your work clearly so that the community can benefit.

Please inform me when your project is publicly available. I would be
glad to see the results.

Best regards,\
Marat Fayzullin\
Author of fMSX
